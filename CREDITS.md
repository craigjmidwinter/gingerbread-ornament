## Built-in Bambu Lab printer settings
The add-on ships `bambu_default_settings.json`, the Bambu Lab P1S (0.4 mm nozzle) printer, process and filament settings in the form Bambu Studio 02.08.04.61 saves them in a project. Every 3MF the add-on exports carries a copy, so Bambu Studio opens it ready to slice.

- These settings come from the system profiles of **Bambu Studio** (https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio), © Bambu Lab, licensed **AGPL-3.0**. They include Bambu's printer start and filament-change G-code templates.
- Section 13 of the GPL-3.0 explicitly allows a GPL-3.0 work to be combined with an AGPL-3.0 work and conveyed. That part stays under AGPL-3.0.
- The process uses Bambu's "0.20mm Standard" profile with a few changes: ironing, prime tower and support type, and wall generator. The filaments are the stock "Bambu PLA Matte @BBL P1S 0.4 nozzle" and "Generic PLA" presets.
- "Bambu Lab", "Bambu Studio", "P1S" and "AMS" are trademarks of Bambu Lab. This add-on is not made or endorsed by Bambu Lab.
